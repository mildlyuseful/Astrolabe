# Trademark and naming policy

The Apache License 2.0 and the CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 - Weakly Reciprocal grant broad
rights to use, modify, and redistribute this project's code and design source. Neither grants rights
to the names or logos the project is identified by. Apache-2.0 section 6 says this explicitly.

## The marks

- **Astrolabe** — the product name.
- **Mildly Useful** — the publisher name and future company identity.
- The Astrolabe and Mildly Useful logos and other distinctive visual identity.

These are used as project and product identifiers. No trademark registration is claimed at this
time, and this document is a statement of how the maintainer intends the names to be used — it is
not a registration and it does not create rights beyond those the law already provides.

## Use that needs no permission

- Saying truthfully that your work uses, integrates with, extends, is compatible with, or is derived
  from Astrolabe.
- Redistributing the project unmodified, under its own name, with its notices intact.
- Discussing, reviewing, teaching, packaging documentation for, or reporting on the project.
- Keeping the existing name references inside source files you have not changed.

Use plain nominative wording: "an add-on for Astrolabe", not "Astrolabe Pro".

## Use that needs permission

- Naming a modified build, fork, distribution, or installer "Astrolabe", or naming it in a way a
  user could mistake for the official build.
- Using the names or logos as, or as part of, your own product, company, service, domain, app-store
  listing, or social account name.
- Using the logos in modified form, or in a way that implies sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation,
  or official status.
- Merchandise.

If you distribute a modified build, please give it your own name and state that it is based on
Astrolabe. That is the point of the policy: users seeing the Astrolabe name should be able to rely
on it identifying the official build, especially once builds are signed and carry a publisher
identity.

## Asking

Open an issue at <https://github.com/mildlyuseful/Astrolabe/issues> describing the intended use. A
contact address will be listed here once the Mildly Useful domain exists.
